# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Please read it in full. The short version, in our own words:

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Welcome questions. There are no stupid questions here.
- Respect that people contribute in different languages, on different devices, with different
  amounts of time and data.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind, including about gender,
  ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, age, or experience level.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing back after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately at
**[contact email to be added]**. Reports are kept confidential. You will get a reply within a
few days.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who break this code, after
following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat groups — and when
someone is representing the project in public, including at events.
